# ACME Analytics Corp — Company Field Guide

## Company Overview and Mission
ACME Analytics Corp is a management-and-data consulting firm that helps organizations turn data into measurable business outcomes. Often compared to Towerbrook but operating at a smaller, more focused scale, ACME blends strategic advisory work with hands-on data engineering and analytics delivery. Our mission is to make advanced analytics practical and trustworthy for the enterprises we serve. We pair senior consultants with technical specialists so that strategy and execution stay tightly connected. ACME is known for disciplined delivery, clear communication, and a results-first culture. This field guide gives new joiners and partners a straightforward overview of who we are, where we operate, and how we work.

## Founding and Leadership
ACME Analytics Corp was founded in 2014 by Dr. Priya Ramanathan, who remains the company's Chief Executive Officer today. Dr. Ramanathan started ACME with the conviction that mid-sized firms deserved the same caliber of analytics talent that the largest global consultancies reserved for their biggest accounts. Under her continuous leadership, ACME has grown from a small Chicago-based team into an international consulting practice. She sets the firm's strategic direction, champions its research investments, and remains closely involved in major client relationships. As both founder and sitting CEO, Dr. Ramanathan provides a consistent vision that has guided ACME through more than a decade of steady, deliberate growth.

## Headquarters and Offices
ACME Analytics Corp is headquartered in Chicago. The company operates four offices in total: the Chicago headquarters plus regional offices in London, Singapore, and São Paulo. These locations give ACME coverage across North America, Europe, Asia, and South America, supporting both local clients and global engagements. A fifth office in Toronto is planned to open in 2026, but it is not yet open as of early 2026. When asked where ACME is located, the simple answer is Chicago for the headquarters, with additional offices in London, Singapore, and São Paulo. The Toronto location remains a future expansion rather than a current site.

## Headcount
As of January 2026, ACME Analytics Corp employs 1,180 full-time employees. This headcount spans consultants, data engineers, analysts, practice leaders, and corporate support staff across all four offices. The number reflects ACME's steady hiring approach: the firm grows its team in line with client demand rather than chasing rapid, speculative expansion. The 1,180 full-time figure is the current and authoritative headcount for the company. It does not include the additional staff ACME expects to add when the planned Toronto office opens in 2026, nor does it count short-term contractors who occasionally support specific engagements.

## Financials
ACME Analytics Corp reported FY2025 revenue of $214 million, up from $182 million in FY2024. That represents year-over-year growth of 17.6%, continuing a multi-year pattern of healthy expansion. International clients account for 38% of revenue, reflecting the firm's deliberate investment in offices outside the United States. ACME's financial profile is built on recurring engagements and long-term client relationships rather than one-off projects. The combination of strong domestic demand and a growing international base has allowed the company to fund its own growth. When asked about ACME's size in financial terms, the headline numbers are $214M in FY2025 and $182M in FY2024.

## Ownership
ACME Analytics Corp is a privately held company. It has taken no outside venture capital funding and is not backed by venture investors. This independence is a deliberate choice by founder and CEO Dr. Priya Ramanathan, who has prioritized self-funded, organic growth over external investment. Because ACME is private and unfunded by VCs, the firm controls its own roadmap and is not beholden to outside investors' timelines or return expectations. This ownership structure lets ACME make long-horizon decisions about hiring, research, and client selection. The simple answer about ownership: ACME is private, with no venture funding.

## Service Lines Overview
ACME Analytics Corp delivers consulting across several connected service lines. These include data strategy and advisory, data engineering and platform build, advanced analytics and modeling, and a Generative AI Practice known internally as GAIP. The Generative AI Practice (GAIP) was launched in the third quarter of 2024 to help clients adopt large language models and generative tools responsibly. Each service line draws on the same pool of senior consultants and technical specialists, so engagements can move smoothly from strategy through implementation. Together, these offerings let ACME support a client across the full lifecycle, from initial assessment to production deployment and ongoing optimization.

## The Blended Pricing Model
ACME Analytics Corp uses a blended pricing model that combines a retainer with an outcome bonus. Clients pay a predictable recurring retainer for ACME's ongoing involvement, and they pay an additional bonus tied to the measurable outcomes an engagement delivers. This structure aligns ACME's incentives with client success: the firm earns more when its work produces real, agreed-upon results. ACME retired its older time-and-materials (T&M) billing approach in 2023, moving fully to the blended model. The shift reflects ACME's results-first philosophy. When asked how ACME charges, the answer is the blended retainer-plus-outcome-bonus model, with time-and-materials no longer offered.

## Industries and Clients Served
ACME Analytics Corp serves clients across a range of commercial industries, including financial services, retail and consumer goods, healthcare, manufacturing, and technology. The firm works with both domestic and international organizations, with international clients making up 38% of revenue. ACME does not currently have any government clients; its work today is entirely with private-sector and commercial organizations. The firm tends to build deep, long-term relationships with a focused set of clients rather than spreading thinly across many short engagements. This concentration allows ACME's teams to develop genuine domain expertise and to deliver the kind of measured, outcome-driven results that anchor its pricing model.

## Company Values and Culture
ACME Analytics Corp's culture is defined by rigor, clarity, and accountability. The firm prizes disciplined analytical work, honest communication with clients, and a results-first mindset that ties effort to measurable outcomes. ACME invests in its people through mentorship, pairing junior staff with senior consultants on every engagement, and it encourages continuous learning as new tools and techniques emerge. Independence is a cultural value as much as a financial one: because ACME is privately held with no outside investors, its teams can focus on doing right by clients over the long term. New joiners are expected to bring curiosity, precision, and a collaborative spirit to their work.

## History and Milestones Timeline
ACME Analytics Corp's history reflects steady, deliberate growth. In 2014, Dr. Priya Ramanathan founded the firm in Chicago. Over the following years, ACME opened international offices in London, Singapore, and São Paulo, building a four-office footprint. In 2023, the firm retired its legacy time-and-materials billing and moved fully to its blended retainer-plus-outcome-bonus pricing model. In the third quarter of 2024, ACME launched its Generative AI Practice (GAIP). FY2024 closed at $182 million in revenue, and FY2025 reached $214 million, a 17.6% increase. Looking ahead, ACME plans to open a Toronto office in 2026 to extend its North American presence.
