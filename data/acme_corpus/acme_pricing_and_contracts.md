# ACME Analytics Corp — Pricing & Contract Terms

## Blended Pricing Philosophy

ACME Analytics Corp moved to a blended pricing model in 2023, retiring pure time-and-materials (T&M) billing across all engagement types. Under the prior T&M regime, clients were exposed to open-ended hourly accruals with weak incentives for consultants to deliver outcomes efficiently; ACME found that T&M arrangements rewarded effort rather than results and complicated revenue forecasting. The blended model now combines a fixed monthly retainer with an outcome-based bonus tied to measurable success metrics agreed at engagement kickoff. This structure aligns ACME's compensation with client value realization, stabilizes cash flow for both parties, and removes the administrative burden of contested timesheets. All engagements signed after 1 January 2024 are governed by this blended model under one of three standard SKUs.

## Enterprise Tier (ACM-ENT-2024)

The Enterprise Tier is provisioned under SKU `ACM-ENT-2024`. The fixed monthly retainer is $85,000 per month, billed in advance. An outcome bonus of up to 20% of the monthly retainer may be earned upon attainment of the success metrics defined in the Statement of Work. The minimum committed term is 12 months. ACME assigns a dedicated team of 8 practitioners, including a named Engagement Director. Support is provided under a 4-hour response SLA for priority incidents during contracted hours. This tier is intended for clients requiring sustained, cross-functional transformation programs.

## Professional Tier (ACM-PRO-2024)

The Professional Tier is provisioned under SKU `ACM-PRO-2024`. The fixed monthly retainer is $45,000 per month, billed in advance. An outcome bonus of up to 15% of the monthly retainer may be earned upon attainment of the success metrics defined in the Statement of Work. The minimum committed term is 6 months. ACME assigns a dedicated team of 5 practitioners, including a named Engagement Lead. Support is provided under an 8-hour response SLA for priority incidents during contracted hours. This tier is intended for clients requiring focused, departmental analytics initiatives.

## Team Tier (ACM-TEAM-2024)

The Team Tier is provisioned under SKU `ACM-TEAM-2024`. The fixed monthly retainer is $20,000 per month, billed in advance. An outcome bonus of up to 10% of the monthly retainer may be earned upon attainment of the success metrics defined in the Statement of Work. The minimum committed term is 3 months. ACME assigns a dedicated team of 3 practitioners, including a named Project Manager. Support is provided under a next-business-day response SLA for priority incidents during contracted hours. This tier is intended for clients requiring scoped, single-workstream support.

## Outcome Bonus Calculation — Enterprise

For Enterprise engagements under `ACM-ENT-2024`, the outcome bonus is computed against three weighted success metrics defined in the Statement of Work. The full bonus of up to 20% of the monthly retainer is payable only when the blended attainment score reaches 90% or higher across the measurement period. Where the blended attainment score falls between 70% and 89%, a pro-rated bonus is payable on a straight-line basis. No outcome bonus accrues where the blended attainment score is below 70%. Attainment is measured quarterly and reconciled at the end of each contract year.

## Outcome Bonus Calculation — Professional

For Professional engagements under `ACM-PRO-2024`, the outcome bonus is computed against three weighted success metrics defined in the Statement of Work. The full bonus of up to 15% of the monthly retainer is payable only when the blended attainment score reaches 85% or higher across the measurement period. Where the blended attainment score falls between 65% and 84%, a pro-rated bonus is payable on a straight-line basis. No outcome bonus accrues where the blended attainment score is below 65%. Attainment is measured quarterly and reconciled at the end of each contract year.

## Early Termination — Enterprise

A client holding an Enterprise Tier (`ACM-ENT-2024`) agreement may terminate for convenience by providing 90 days' written notice. Upon early termination, an early-exit fee applies equal to 50% of the remaining retainer payments due through the end of the minimum committed term, plus any outcome bonus already accrued and unpaid. Termination for cause, following ACME's failure to cure a material breach within the contractual cure period, incurs no early-exit fee. Notice must be delivered to ACME's contracts office in Chicago.

## Early Termination — Professional

A client holding a Professional Tier (`ACM-PRO-2024`) agreement may terminate for convenience by providing 30 days' written notice. Upon early termination, an early-exit fee applies equal to 25% of the remaining retainer payments due through the end of the minimum committed term, plus any outcome bonus already accrued and unpaid. Termination for cause, following ACME's failure to cure a material breach within the contractual cure period, incurs no early-exit fee. Notice must be delivered to ACME's contracts office in Chicago.

## Data Processing Agreement (DPA) Terms

Every ACME engagement that involves the processing of client personal data is governed by a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) executed as an addendum to the Master Services Agreement. Under the DPA, ACME acts as a data processor and the client as data controller. ACME commits to processing personal data only on documented instructions from the client, to maintaining technical and organizational security measures consistent with ISO 27001, and to notifying the client of any personal data breach within 48 hours of becoming aware. Sub-processors must be disclosed in advance and are subject to client objection rights. Cross-border transfers from ACME's London, Singapore, and São Paulo offices rely on Standard Contractual Clauses where required. The DPA survives termination of the underlying engagement for as long as ACME retains client personal data.

## Payment Schedule and Net-45 Invoicing

Monthly retainers are invoiced in advance on the first business day of each calendar month. Outcome bonuses are invoiced in arrears following quarterly attainment reconciliation. All ACME invoices are issued on Net-45 terms, meaning payment is due within 45 calendar days of the invoice date. Invoices unpaid after 45 days accrue late interest at 1.5% per month on the outstanding balance. Invoices are denominated in the currency of the contracting office: USD for Chicago, GBP for London, SGD for Singapore, and BRL for São Paulo. Disputed invoice line items must be raised in writing within 15 calendar days of receipt; undisputed amounts remain due on the original schedule.

## Multi-Year Commitment Discount Policy

Clients committing to engagement terms beyond the standard tier minimums qualify for retainer discounts applied to the fixed monthly retainer. A 24-month commitment earns a 7.5% discount on the monthly retainer. A 36-month commitment earns a 12% discount on the monthly retainer. Discounts apply only to the retainer component and never to the outcome bonus, which is always computed against the undiscounted retainer figure. Multi-year discounts may not be combined with promotional pricing and are forfeited if the client exercises early termination for convenience before the committed term concludes.

## Generative AI Practice (GAIP) Add-On Pricing

The Generative AI Practice (GAIP) is available as an add-on module to any tier. GAIP add-on pricing is charged at an incremental 25% of the base monthly retainer for the contracted tier and includes a dedicated AI solutions architect. GAIP engagements are subject to the same outcome bonus and attainment mechanics as the underlying tier, with success metrics specific to model deployment and adoption. The GAIP add-on requires an executed DPA addendum covering any client data used in model fine-tuning or evaluation.

## Renewal and Price Adjustment

Engagements renew automatically at the end of the committed term for successive 12-month periods unless either party provides written non-renewal notice at least 60 days before term expiry. At each renewal, ACME may adjust the fixed monthly retainer by no more than the greater of 4% or the prior-year change in the relevant regional consumer price index. Outcome bonus percentages and attainment thresholds remain fixed for the life of the engagement unless renegotiated in a signed amendment.
